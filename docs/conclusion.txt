Originally everything ran in one loop — when MQTT tried to reconnect, 
it blocked the sensor reads, and any readings during that time were 
just lost. 

I refactored into separate FreeRTOS tasks: one reads sensors and queues 
data, another handles Wi‑Fi/MQTT. During a simulated 90‑second broker 
outage, sensor sampling stayed on schedule, the queue held 51 readings
without dropping any, and when the broker came back, everything was sent. 
Zero data lost.
